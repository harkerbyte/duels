# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience, background or identity.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it. To report a problem, message [@shade_ofc](https://x.com/shade_ofc) on X.
